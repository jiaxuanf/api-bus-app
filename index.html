How the times are worked out. Bus arrivals at your boarding stop are live from arrivelah2, refreshed every 20 seconds. Everything after you board is estimated, not live: riding is modelled at 18 km/h along the real route path (that is the SG average once dwell time at stops is counted), walking at 4.7 km/h with a 1.35× detour factor because you cannot walk through buildings, and a transfer assumes a 6 minute wait for the connecting bus. Treat the arrival clock as a good estimate, not a promise.

Trains are modelled end to end. Singapore publishes no live train-arrival feed, so nothing on an MRT leg is measured: 45 km/h including dwell, a 4 minute wait to board and 4 minutes to change lines. That is also why a train leg never tells you to run — inventing urgency from missing data would be worse than saying nothing. LRT is left out: the Bukit Panjang, Sengkang and Punggol lines are loops, and station-code order does not describe which way a loop runs.
